# Next steps

*Plain language: what each version adds, so v1.0 is understood as a first step.*

- **v0.x (now to December 2026):** equilibrium calculator, codebook, extraction protocol and quality checks public on GitHub; first papers extracted and checked.
- **Next extraction rounds (October to December 2026):** digitize figures for the 8 deferred papers in `data/extraction/screening_log.csv` (first: the microwave PtSn/SiO2 paper, 10.1126/sciadv.adi8219); screen the remaining 81 open-access Europe PMC hits; add subscription papers the maintainer can access; ask authors of electrified-heating papers for tabulated values.
- **v1.0 (target January 2027):** the extracted dataset, codebook, provenance log and CC BY 4.0 license archived on Zenodo with a DOI.
- **v1.1 (target June 2027):** equilibrium-normalized comparison layer documented and released: approach-to-equilibrium, the common deactivation constant, and computed space velocities, with a figure placing every catalyst against the ceiling.
- **Later:** a short data paper (for example *Scientific Data* or *Data in Brief*); a contribution template so other labs can submit their own results; separate ceilings for membrane and chemical-looping reactors; a full-equilibrium variant including cracking products.
