# Changelog

## v1.0.0 (2026-10-19)

First public release.

- 153 reported test points for 51 catalysts from 17 peer-reviewed papers (2017 to 2026), all checked against the source papers by the curator.
- Heating modes: conventional, induction (magnetically induced) and radio-frequency.
- Equilibrium layer: single-reaction ceiling from NASA TM-4513 data for every row; 18 of 18 physics checks pass, including agreement with NIST heats of formation and with an independent property library.
- Comparison columns: approach to equilibrium, above-ceiling flag, propylene yield, computed space velocity, deactivation constant on one common definition.
- Quoted-equilibrium check: 11 equilibrium values printed in papers compared with the calculator; 5 differ by more than 2 percentage points.
- Codebook, extraction protocol with decision record, provenance log, search and screening logs, QA reports.
